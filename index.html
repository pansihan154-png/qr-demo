It’s a demo for a security awareness session on QR code phishing. This page has no forms and no tracking, and it collects nothing about you.

The next one might not be.

In 2023, a woman in Singapore scanned a QR code on a sticker at a bubble tea shop to fill in a survey for a free drink. The code led to malware on her phone, and she lost about S$20,000 while she was asleep.

Three habits

Before you scan

Look for a sticker stuck over something else. Ask yourself whether you expected a code here at all.

After you scan

Read the address before you type anything. Use your phone’s own camera app, not a third-party scanner.

Codes that log you in

Only scan a login or approval code inside the official app or website. Never scan one that someone sends you.
